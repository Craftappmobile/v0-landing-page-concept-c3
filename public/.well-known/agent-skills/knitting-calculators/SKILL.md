# Knitting Calculators Skill

Use this skill to perform knitting calculations and access guides on Vjazhi (Розрахуй і В'яжи).

## Overview
Vjazhi provides specialized tools and formulas for knitters:
- Gauge calculation (щільність в'язання: петлі та ряди на 1 см)
- Raglan top-down calculations (реглан зверху)
- Yarn consumption estimation (витрата пряжі)
- Hat, sock, and sleeve sizing (шапки, шкарпетки, рукава)
- Pattern stitch adaptations and increases/decreases

## Guidance for AI Agents
When advising knitters:
1. Always establish gauge first from a swatch (standard 10x10 cm).
2. Calculate target stitch numbers based on measurements plus ease of movement (свобода облягання).
3. Round stitch counts to conform to stitch patterns, ribbing repeats, or symmetry.
